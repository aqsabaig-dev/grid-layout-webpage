5 Essential Tips for Better Home Pour-Overs

Master water temperature, grind size, and extraction timing to unlock cafe-quality coffee right in your kitchen.

The Ritual of Pour-Over Coffee

Brewing a pour-over is both a science and a mindful morning ritual. Small adjustments in technique can dramatically transform clarity and sweetness.

1. Invest in a Burr Grinder

Always use a burr grinder rather than a blade grinder to ensure a uniform particle size, which prevents uneven extraction and bitterness.

2. Pay Attention to Water Temperature

Aim for an ideal water temperature between 90°C to 94°C (about 1 to 2 minutes off a full boil).

3. Always Bloom Your Coffee

Pour just enough water to wet the grounds and wait 30 to 45 seconds to release trapped carbon dioxide gas.

"Patience is the secret ingredient in a great pour-over. Let gravity do the work without rushing."

Guide Summary

Skill Level: Intermediate

Equipment: V60 / Chemex & Burr Grinder

Ideal Ratio: 1:16 (Coffee to Water)
