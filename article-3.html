Light Roast vs Dark Roast Explained

Understand the science behind roast profiles, acidity levels, and flavor retention.

Decoding the Roasting Spectrum

Choosing between a light roast and a dark roast often comes down to personal taste. However, the difference goes far deeper than color.

Light Roast: Preserving Origin Characteristics

Light roasts retain the most authentic characteristics of the bean's origin with bright acidity and floral/fruity notes.

Dark Roast: Boldness and Caramelization

Dark roasts stay in the roaster longer, bringing out smoky, bold, and chocolatey flavors with lower acidity.

"Neither roast style is superior; each offers a completely different lens through which to experience coffee."

Quick Comparison

Light Roast: High acidity, lighter body, origin flavors.


Dark Roast: Low acidity, heavy body, smoky/chocolatey flavors.
