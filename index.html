WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Passion, Brewed to Perfection.

Founded in 2016, Brew & Bean started with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between ethical coffee farmers and true coffee enthusiasts. We travel directly to high-altitude estates across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil to handpick the finest green beans.

Our master roasters treat every batch as a work of art, carefully monitoring temperature and time to unlock the unique flavor notes, rich body, and irresistible aroma of each bean.
